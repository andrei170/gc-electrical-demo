Landlords & Agents

EICRs, on a schedule, before you get chased for one

Rented property in England needs an electrical safety certificate every five years. Missing one is a fine you do not want and a tenancy you cannot start.

Landlord service

One property or a portfolio

  • EICR inspection and certificate, issued electronically
  • Remedial work quoted separately — never bundled in without asking
  • Renewal reminders before the certificate expires
  • Direct with tenants for access, so you are not the middleman
  • One invoice across multiple properties
  • Same electrician each visit, who knows the buildings

Why it is worth setting up properly

  • An expired EICR can stop a tenancy starting on the day it should
  • Remedials found early are cheaper than remedials found at check-in
  • Certificates stored and reissued when your agent asks for them
  • A known electrician beats whoever is available in an emergency
  • Scheduling several properties together keeps the cost down
